agency owner deciding whether to email Nic about fractional CTO / advisory
work; a new reader deciding whether his field notes are worth their time.
Research: `research/about-pages.md`. Voice: `.writings-memory/memory.md`.

```

name: Nic Alpi
role: CTO at Amba · development and product
also: board advisor to software agencies
before: built and sold a Rails consultancy (2011 → 2021)
based: Bristol, UK · French, here since 2008
writes: field notes on leading teams and AI without losing ownership

```

1. **Opening moment** — two options for Nic to pick:
   - A: 2021 — selling the agency he'd spent ten years building and becoming
     CTO of the company that bought it. What changed about the job.
   - B: January 2026 — freezing the hiring plan to understand AI's effect on
     the team before adding people; what that says about how he works.
2. **What I do now** — Amba: development and product, 8 people, software for
   elderly care; board advisor to agencies. Plain, specific, no adjectives.
3. **What I've learned the hard way** — the beliefs, in prose, 3–4 sentences;
   the sustainable-pace idea reframed as design, not lifestyle.
4. **Why I publish field notes** — the "certainty the author doesn't have"
   line; what the notes cover; one line inviting the reader to start.
5. **Working with me** — one paragraph: the two shapes (fractional, advisory),
   who it suits, "I've run teams through this before — you borrow that".
6. **End on the next step** — the email address, one sentence. No summary.
