## Six things I believe after 20 years.

Most technical leaders don't need another tool. They need clearer attention, better decisions and stronger follow-through.

- The best CTOs aren't the busiest. They've designed systems, teams and lives that don't require heroics.
- Boring technology wins. Rails has been declared dead for 15 years. Still ships faster.
- Complexity is a choice. Most teams have simplicity problems, not scaling problems.
- Unexamined meetings become debt. A recurring meeting nobody questions taxes deep thinking.
- AI made output cheap, not judgement. Attention before automation.
- Burnout exposes a systems problem. If your company needs heroes, your systems are broken.
