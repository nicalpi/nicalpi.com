A bit more about me...

I'm not going to write a third-person bio. That would be weird. I'm a French developer who moved to the UK in 2008. In 2011, my wife and I started a Rails consultancy. We grew it to 20 people and over a million in revenue before selling to Amba in 2021.

Now I'm CTO at Amba, running an 8-person dev team building software for elderly care. I train HYROX at 5:30am. I have a hard 4pm rule — phone down, family time, non-negotiable.

I write because I kept seeing advice from navy seals and billionaires performing certainty they don't have. I'm not any of those things. I'm genuinely confused about AI, I sometimes ship things late, and I'm still figuring this out. I figure that's more useful to write about.

— Nic

What I've learned

Six things I believe after 20 years.

The best CTOs aren't the busiest.

They've designed systems, teams, and lives that don't require heroics.

Boring technology wins.

Rails has been declared dead for 15 years. Still ships faster than most alternatives.

Complexity is a choice.

Most teams don't have scaling problems. They have simplicity problems.

Meetings are debt.

Every recurring meeting is a tax on your team's ability to think deeply.

AI changes the game, not the fundamentals.

Context engineering — treating AI like a junior who needs proper onboarding.

Burnout is a systems problem.

If your company needs heroes to function, your systems are broken.
