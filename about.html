A bit more about me...

I'm Nic Alpi.

I'm not going to write a third-person bio. That would be weird. I'm a French developer who moved to the UK in 2008. In 2011, my wife and I started a Rails consultancy called CookiesHQ. We grew it to 20 people and over a million in revenue before selling to Amba in 2021.

Now I'm CTO at Amba, running an 8-person dev team building software for elderly care. It's meaningful work. I train HYROX at 5:30am. I have 3 kids. I have a hard 4pm rule — phone down, family time, non-negotiable.

I write because I kept seeing advice from navy seals and billionaires performing certainty they don't have. I'm not any of those things. I'm genuinely confused about AI, I sometimes ship things late, and I'm still figuring this out. I figure that's more useful to write about.

I live in Bristol with my family. I'm usually reading, walking, or trying to remember that not everything needs to be optimised.

— Nic
